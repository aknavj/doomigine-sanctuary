Sanctuary data subset loaded by id Tech 4.

sun as a projected light, omni/projected lights, particles, music,
nine md5camera paths with the demo's 20-frame clamp and a short fade.
Window lights and editor-hidden lights are present but hidden.
Flame and candle lights are forced on so the hall is not black.
Needs the dhewm3-cupt ASE loader fix: stock Doom 3 parses *MESH_VERTCOL as zero, which blacks out vertexColor.
Not included: parallax, glow, HDR, DOF, stereo, LUTs, cubemap reflections,
transmission, and a real directional sun. The path tracer was not modified.
